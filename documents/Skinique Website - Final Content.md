## 1. About the Clinic

Skinique is a dermatology and aesthetic medicine clinic founded by Dr. Kajal Komalan. We believe good skin and hair care isn't about doing the most, it's about doing what's right for you. Every patient who walks through our doors gets a plan built around their specific skin, concerns, and goals, not a standard package applied to everyone.

That philosophy shapes how we treat, too: we keep every protocol only as extensive as it needs to be, favoring the simplest effective approach over piling on treatments for their own sake.

#### 1. PRP Hair Therapy

*Raw notes from client, stored verbatim (not yet drafted into final polished copy):*

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) is a treatment that uses your own blood. A small sample is collected and centrifuged to concentrate the platelets and growth factors. The platelet-rich plasma is then injected into the scalp to support hair growth and improve hair density.

**Who it's suitable for:**
- Male Pattern Hair Loss (MPHL)
- Female Pattern Hair Loss (FPHL)
- Diffuse Hair Fall
- Alopecia Areata (AA)
- Overall Hair & Scalp Rejuvenation

**Treatment Specs — Duration, Downtime, Sessions & Results:**
- Duration: Approximately 1–2 hours
- Downtime: Approximately 24 hours
- Sessions: A series of 6–8 sessions may be recommended, depending on the individual's hair loss and treatment response
- Results: Visible improvement may begin after 3–6 sessions, varying depending on the underlying cause and individual response
